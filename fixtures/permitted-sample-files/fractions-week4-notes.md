# Grade 4 Mathematics - Unit Fractions (Week 4)

## Lesson objective

Students build non-unit fractions from unit fractions using fraction strips, and explain why
3/4 is three copies of 1/4.

## Warm-up (5 minutes)

Ask students to fold a paper strip into 4 equal parts. Label one part 1/4. Ask: how many 1/4
pieces make the whole strip?

## Main activity (20 minutes)

1. Distribute the fraction-strip worksheet (see `fractions-worksheet.pdf`).
2. Students shade the number of unit fractions shown in each problem and write the resulting
   fraction (e.g., shading three 1/4 strips gives 3/4).
3. Circulate and check that students count the shaded unit fractions rather than guessing.
4. Review problems 1-4 as a class; have a student explain problem 5 aloud.

## Independent practice (10 minutes)

Students complete problems 6-10 on the worksheet individually.

## Materials

- Fraction strip worksheet (see attached PDF)
- Pre-cut paper strips (1 per student, from the classroom supply bin)

## Vocabulary

unit fraction, numerator, denominator, equal parts
