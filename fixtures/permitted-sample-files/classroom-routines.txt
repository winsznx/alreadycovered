Classroom routines - Grade 4A (illustrative fixture, teacher-authored)

# Arrival
Students line up outside the room and enter quietly, placing backpacks in their assigned cubby
before sitting at their desk for morning attendance.

# Materials
Each table group has a shared supply bin (pencils, erasers, glue sticks). Students return
borrowed supplies to the bin before transitions.

# Dismissal
Students pack up at the 2-minute warning bell, stack chairs, and line up by the door in table
group order before being released.
